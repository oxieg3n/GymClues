From Home, tap Preview session to review today's assigned workout.

Check the exercises and targets, then tap Start workout.

Your session includes editable weights, reps, and set logging.

Return Home, then scroll down to Rest Day.

Tap Rest Day when you need a recovery day.

Set Today as Rest Day pauses training and moves your program forward.

Your workout moves to the next training day.

Open Rest Day again to plan repeating breaks.

Choose Plan Rest Day for your regular schedule.

Under Every week, select the weekdays you want off.

Weekly rest days repeat automatically and save immediately.

Use Add date for a one-off rest day.

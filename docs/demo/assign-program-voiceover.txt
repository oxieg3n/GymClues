To assign a workout program, open the menu.

Choose Pick a Program to browse your options.

Choose a training style that fits your goals and schedule.

Compare the routines, then open Details for a closer look.

Review the experience level, training cycle, and how the program progresses.

Tap Assign to me when you've found the right fit.

Choose Today, Tomorrow, or Next Monday as your start date.

Tap Assign to save your start date.

Already Assigned confirms your selection and prevents duplicate assignments.

Use the back arrow to return to Home.

Today's training now follows your program, with a preview and a button to start.

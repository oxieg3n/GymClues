# <h1>GymClues</h1>
<h3>Less guesswork. More time for your workout.</h3>

<b>GymClues</b> helps you figure out the equipment at your gym and put together your workout. Browse exercises by muscle group or workout style, follow a beginner-friendly plan, or save your own. You can easily switch exercises when a machine is busy, log your lift weight and reps, and it even keeps Spotify within reach. <br>
<br>Oh, and your gym isn’t listed? Add it and its equipment to help the next person out.

<h1><b></b>Updated Features</h1>
<h2>Workout Programs and Custom Workouts</h2><br>
Bundled workout templates grouped by training split.<br>
Create, edit, reorder, and delete custom workouts.<br>
Configure sets, target reps, and rest periods per exercise.<br>
Log weight and reps, check off completed sets, and automatically start a rest countdown.<br>
Adjust sets during workouts; add suggested exercises or choose from the library.<br>
Automatically substitute alternatives when configured equipment is unavailable.<br>
Save a modified session as a custom workout without changing the original plan.<br><br>

<h2>Monitor Your Consistency</h2><br>
Home dashboard with weekly streaks and a seven-day activity strip.<br>
Workout-day totals for the current week, month, and year.<br>
Workout history grouped by day.<br>
Total training volume and last-seven-days volume.<br>
Charts for daily workout volume and each exercise’s highest logged weight.<br>
Daily summaries showing sets, volume, and best weight per exercise. Counts treat a day with logged sets as one workout day.<br><br>

<H2>Learn How To Properly Use Equipment</H2><br>
Browse exercises by muscle group.<br>
Exercise guides covering setup, execution, common mistakes, breathing, and safety.<br>
“Machine busy?” alternatives.<br>
Equipment availability toggles that filter exercise options.<br>
Equipment pages with photos, alternate names, related exercises, and usage instructions.<br>
Exercise-specific equipment setup: angles, seat positions, attachments, and additional equipment.<br>
Embedded YouTube/MP4 tutorials(coming soon)<br><br>

<h2>Find Your Gym And Update Its Available Equipment</h2><br>
Find gyms using GPS or name/city search.<br>
Add gyms and select your active training location.<br>
View gym equipment inventories and submission statuses.<br>
Add equipment using camera/gallery photos and QR scanning.<br>
Record manufacturer, model, muscles worked, instructions, video links, and notes.<br>
Save drafts, edit submissions, and view reviewer feedback.<br>
Fill missing details from confident local catalog matches.<br><br>

<h2>Media Contorls</h2><br>
Control your music/podcast directly from the GymClues app<br><br>
